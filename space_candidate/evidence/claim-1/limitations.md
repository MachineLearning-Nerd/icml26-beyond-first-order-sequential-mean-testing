# Claim 1 limitations and deviations

This is finite-sample corroboration at the paper's two illustrative distributions, not an exhaustive proof for every bounded distribution. The exact paper sample-size grid is not recoverable from the HTML text; the grid 50, 200, 1,000, and 5,000 tests convergence and retains a deliberately non-Gaussian small-n control. No theorem-derived sample size or tolerance is used to select the largest n.
