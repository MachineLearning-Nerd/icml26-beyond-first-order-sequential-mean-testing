# Claim 2 limitations

This experiment is finite corroboration for one exact non-toy distribution, not a proof over every `q ∈ Q_bd`. Formal alpha labels below floating-point range are represented by their exact `b=log(1/alpha)` values, which are all the algorithm and normalization require. The constant boundary is reported only as a practical comparator and does not inherit the growing boundary's time-uniform guarantee.
