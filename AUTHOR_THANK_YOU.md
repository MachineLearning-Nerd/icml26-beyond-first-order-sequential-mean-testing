# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Vikas Deep and Shubhada Agrawal for sharing *Beyond First-order Asymptotics in Sequential Mean Testing* and for developing a careful second-order analysis of KL_inf-based sequential mean tests.

This repository is an independent reproduction and audit. The claim contracts, finite evidence, corrections, and limitations are documented so that readers can inspect the work without confusing a reproduction result with authorship of the original paper.
